# Privacy

Sharable Bible Reading Plan is an instruction-only plugin. It does not operate a separate database or server.

When a reader connects Google Drive, the plugin creates and updates a **My Bible Reading Journey** Sheet in that reader's own Google Drive. The Sheet may contain reading progress, preferences, questions, and study notes the reader chooses to provide.

The plugin instructs ChatGPT and Codex to:

- use only the current reader's Sheet;
- never request, reveal, or reuse another person's Sheet or reading history;
- never share a Sheet unless its owner explicitly asks;
- avoid inventing personal responses, interests, or time spent.

Access to Google Drive is controlled through the reader's OpenAI and Google connection settings. Readers can revoke that connection or delete their Sheet at any time.
